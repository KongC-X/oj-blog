P4956 [COCI 2017/2018 #6] Davor

洛谷 | 入门 | C++14 | 提交时间: 2025-08-01

算法标签：2017、不定方程、COCI（克罗地亚）

题目描述

After successfully conquering the South Pole, Davor is preparing for new challenges. Next up is the Arctic expedition to Siberia, Greenland and Norway. He begins his travels on 31 December 2018, and needs to collect NNN kunas (Croatian currency) by then. In order to do this, he has decided to put away XXX (X≤100X \le 100X≤100) kunas every Monday to his travel fund, X+KX + KX+K kunas every Tuesday, X+2×KX + 2\times KX+2×K every Wednesday, and so on until Sunday, when he will put away X+6×KX + 6\times KX+6×K kunas. This way, he will collect money for 52 weeks, starting with 1 January 2018 (Monday) until 30 December 2018 (Sunday).

If we know the amount of money NNN, output the values XXX and KKK so that it is possible to collect the exact money amount in the given timespan. The solution will always exist, and if there are multiple, output the one with the greatest XXX and smallest KKK.

输入格式

The first line of input contains the integer NNN (1456≤N≤1456001456\le N \le 1456001456≤N≤145600), the number from the task.

输出格式

The first line of output must contain the value of XXX (0≤X≤1000 \le X \le 1000≤X≤100), and the second the value of KKK (K>0K > 0K>0).

样例

样例输入 #1

1456```

**样例输出 #1**

1
1```

样例输入 #2

6188```

**样例输出 #2**

14
1```

样例输入 #3

40404```

**样例输出 #3**

99
4```
